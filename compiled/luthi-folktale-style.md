# The folktale surface: the style that refuses depth on purpose

*Folded from `luthi-folktale-style.md`, canon V3.3, August 2026. Compiled variant: the source file is master.*

One job: hold the surface the folktale is told on. Not the sequence of events (the Propp file) and not the belief the tale earns (the Tolkien file).

**The one question.** Is the surface doing all the work, or has something started asking the reader to look beneath it?

Five features, five views of a single commitment: the tale lives entirely on its surface. Not passes to run in sequence but one discipline, held while drafting and checked while revising.

Source: Max Lüthi, *The European Folktale: Form and Nature*, translated by John D. Niles (Institute for the Study of Human Issues, 1982; Indiana University Press edition 1986; German original, Francke, 1947). The features and their names are Lüthi's; the drafting discipline, register framing, and examples are the writer's. Examples are for calibration, not for lifting.

## One-dimensionality

The everyday world and the otherworld occupy a single plane. The hero meets a talking fox, a dead man, a witch, and negotiates. No shudder, no vertigo, no pause to register that the impossible has happened. Lüthi's contrast is the local legend, where an otherworld encounter is uncanny and the teller half believes it; in the folktale the marvelous is matter-of-fact, and its beings matter only for what they do.

On the page: introduce otherworld figures by role and transaction, not by wonder. Nobody in the tale asks how the magic works, and neither does the teller.

Fails as imported awe. The moment a draft stops to establish that foxes cannot talk, it has stepped out of the tale's world into ours.

Broken: "She stopped dead. Foxes did not speak; every law of the world said they could not, and yet this one had." Held: "A fox sat by the road and said, 'Give me one of your loaves and I will show you the way.' She gave it a loaf, and it ran ahead of her."

## Depthlessness

Folktale figures are figures, not characters. No rendered inner life, no felt duration, no bodies carrying their history. Feelings and qualities push outward into actions and objects: grief is weeping, envy is a shove into the well, love is a ring. Time passes in a clause and leaves no mark the tale does not need.

On the page: state feeling flatly or convert it to an act. Give motive a clause, if it appears at all. Do not enter a head; report what hands and feet do.

Fails as the novelistic intrusion: a weighed decision, a remembered childhood, a stretch of free indirect style. In this register that intrusion is the register slip the retuned Gardner scan exists to catch.

Broken: "All night the youngest lay awake and turned the betrayal over. When had his brothers first learned to hate him?" Held: "The two elder brothers took the treasure and threw the youngest into the well. He climbed out by the roots of a tree and went on his way."

## The abstract style

The folktale does not depict the world; it transforms it. Outlines sharp, surfaces empty. Colors pure and few: red, white, black, gold. Materials run to metal, glass, and stone. Everything tends toward the extreme and the superlative: the most beautiful princess, the poorest woodcutter, the end of the world. Prohibitions, conditions, and impossible tasks arrive as fixed terms, stated once and binding. Objects are named, never described; a golden apple is exactly that.

On the page: be precise about outline and silent about texture. The specificity this style wants is the concrete image and the plain act, not the proper noun, the date, or the documentary detail. This is the ground for the register's re-scoped specificity floor: the tale is vivid without ever being documentary.

Fails as description creep, realistic texture accumulating until the clean line blurs into scene-setting.

Broken: "a low cottage of weather-silvered pine, its thatch furred with moss, a thread of smoke above the one crooked chimney." Held: "a little house deep in the forest."

## Formula and number

The style's fixed machinery: opening and closing formulas, speeches repeated word for word at each encounter, and formulaic numbers, with three far ahead of seven and twelve. Three brothers, three tasks, three nights. Repetition is exact or escalating, and the third pass carries the change: the third door, the third guess, the third night the watcher keeps his eyes open.

On the page: build in threes and repeat wording verbatim; elegant variation is a literary habit the folktale does not share. Load the change into the third slot.

Fails two ways. Varied repetition, where synonyms dissolve the formula. And the missing third, where a pattern is set twice and abandoned before its payoff.

**The register note.** In argumentative registers the catalog counts triad saturation as a tell (H1.4), and the Forsyth repetition family sets the ceilings. Here the count inverts to a floor: the triad is the folktale's unit of structure, not its ornament. Remove the third brother and you have removed skeleton, not polish. Opening and closing formulas belong to the same inventory; the argument for why the register requires the doorway formula (H2.1 inverted) is the Tolkien file's.

Held: "The first night the eldest kept watch, and he slept. The second night the middle brother kept watch, and he slept. The third night the youngest kept watch, and at midnight the ground opened."

## Isolation and universal interconnection

Folktale figures are cut loose. The hero leaves home alone; ties do not persist; helpers are strangers met once; gifts come from encounters, not relationships. Episodes seal off from each other so completely that the tale tolerates motifs that have lost their purpose, a key that never finds its lock. And this severance is what frees the tale to move: because nothing is bound into a web, anything can connect with anything. What a novel would call coincidence, the tale treats as the ordinary traffic of its world. The unattached hero is the one who can take the fox's bargain and marry the king's daughter.

On the page: encounter, exchange, and onward. No letters home, no standing relationships, no callbacks the form does not require. When a figure has served its purpose, let it go.

Fails as the novelistic web: continuity of acquaintance, consequences tracked across episodes, the helper given a history.

Broken: "As he walked he wondered how his mother fared, and whether the miller still pressed her about the debt." Held: "At the crossroads he met an old man. The old man asked for his coat, and he gave it. The old man gave him an iron key and was gone."

## Sublimation and all-inclusiveness

The folktale takes the heaviest human material, hunger, marriage, cruelty, exile, death, and empties it of concrete weight. The poverty has no economics, the wedding no politics, the punishment no blood the tale lingers on. Sublimated to pure form, motifs turn light and transparent, and that lightness is the point: emptied of any particular reality, the tale can carry any meaning. This is the all-inclusiveness of the pair: the tale can hold the world precisely because it documents none of it.

On the page: let heavy matter pass lightly. Poverty is a premise, death is an event, punishment is a sentence long. The stakes stay; the sociology goes.

Fails as realism creep in the dark material: deprivation documented, wounds rendered, cruelty psychologized. The motif takes its weight back and the form breaks under it.

**The register note.** Sublimation is what makes the doubled spine possible. Allegory doubles McDonald: the surface tale and the carried meaning each need an armature, and it must be the same one. A motif emptied of its particular reality is free to carry that meaning; a motif loaded with sociology insists on itself instead.

Broken: "Hunger had hollowed the children's faces, and the village had long since stopped meeting the woodcutter's eyes." Held: "The woodcutter was so poor that he could no longer feed his children."

## Relation to the other files

**The Gardner file, re-scoped.** Gardner and Lüthi disagree, and here Lüthi governs. Gardner asks for depth on tap and a modulated lens; the folktale forbids both. Gardner's end survives while his means invert: the vivid and continuous dream is still the goal, and the folktale sustains it through the consistency of its flat surface rather than through interiority and closeness. The dream-breaker scan still runs with its catalog swapped: here the register slip is the novelistic intrusion, a motive explained, a feeling rendered from inside, a texture documented. Psychic distance locks at the far setting, and Gardner's flinch against a lens held too long at one distance is inoperative, because the fixed lens is the form. One distinction keeps the files from colliding: Gardner's frigidity, the reader sensing the writer does not care, is not the folktale's flatness. The tale cares through action and outcome, and a tale can be perfectly depthless and fully felt.

**The Forsyth repetition family** owns the mechanics of triads and sets their ceilings for argumentative prose. This file supplies the reason the ceiling becomes a floor here.

**Orwell, Williams, and the scrub** all still run. Depthlessness is a property of the told world, not a discount on the prose. A folktale full of machine lexicon is machine prose in costume, and the gate treats it as such.

**The Propp and Tolkien files** own the function sequence and the doorway formula, secondary belief, and the turn. The three ship as a set.

## Things to flinch at

**Flat is a register, not a default.** Outside this register the depthless style reads as affectless prose. Nothing here licenses exporting it.

**Flat is not careless.** The abstract style is exact: sharp outline, clean formula, the change in the third slot. A limp sentence is not depthless, it is limp. Every prose pass still applies.

**Deliberate hybrids exist.** Literary fairy tales and modern retellings break these features on purpose; interiority in a retelling can be the entire point. This file describes the traditional form. Where a writer breaks it knowingly, the one rule holds.

**Depthless is not weightless.** Sublimation lightens the telling, not the stakes. Abandonment, betrayal, and the impossible task still cut. A draft that flattens consequence along with rendering produces a tale where nothing matters, which is a different failure from depth.

## Scope

Lüthi's book argues past this file: the running contrast between folktale and local legend, the chapter on the style's function and significance, and the survey of folktale scholarship including a supplement on the structural school. His later books extend the argument. The rule of three has a scholarly literature of its own beyond Lüthi. Start with Niles's translation.
